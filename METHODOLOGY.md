# Methodology and AI disclosure

## Purpose
This is a public archive of **substantially AI-generated** long-form analysis. It is intended for critical reading and source inspection, not as a substitute for original reporting or certified government records.

## Production workflow
1. A human supplies the question, goals, and editorial direction.
2. A generative AI assistant researches publicly available reporting, identifies sources, drafts text, and may design visuals.
3. Facts should be checked against named sources. When complete verification has not occurred, the document must be marked **provisional**.
4. Publishing fixes a **dated snapshot** of what was reported, not a definitive statement about still-evolving events.
5. Material errors should be fixed transparently with a changelog; Git preserves revision history.

## Evidence rules
- Link primary official records when available; otherwise use reputable, attributed reporting.
- Distinguish election calls from official certification, vote counts from polling, and explanations from proven causes.
- Mark opinion, forecasts, and secondhand descriptions of voter motivations as such.
- Never present AI-generated reconstructed quotes as statements from real people.
- Cite a source for each important statistical or contentious claim, noting report date and sample when relevant.
- Include counterevidence when it materially changes interpretation.

## Editorial status and known limitations
The first three election reports were generated on **November 4, 2026** using developing election-night and next-day returns. Some source links may be updated or moved. The releases preserve a historical snapshot. **No independent, line-by-line human fact-check has been certified.** Figures and interpretations should therefore be treated as *provisional and independently verified before quotation*. Confirm current election results with state elections officials.

The PDFs are presentation editions automatically rebuilt from the canonical Markdown; styling may differ from the original ChatGPT drafts.
